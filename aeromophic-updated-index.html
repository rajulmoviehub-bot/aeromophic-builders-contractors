Professional credentials

NCA Building Works — Category 7

Aeromophic Construction Works states that it holds NCA Building Works registration in Category 7. Work is undertaken only while the practising licence is valid and within the exact class, scope and conditions shown on current NCA records.

NCA publishes a maximum contract value of KSh 20,000,000 for Building Works Category 7, subject to current NCA rules and a valid practising licence. Clients may request current registration and licence documents for verification. Specialist electrical, mechanical, communications, roads/civil or other separately regulated work may require separately registered or licensed providers.
