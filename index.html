Who We Are

Building trust through quality work.

Aeromophic Construction Works is a Kenyan building and construction business focused on delivering practical, durable and well-finished construction solutions.

From residential homes to suitable institutional projects, we aim to provide reliable service, clear communication and responsible project coordination.

  • Renovations and improvement works

Registration & Compliance

NCA Building Works Category 7

The business identifies its registration as NCA Building Works Category 7. All contracts are subject to verification of the current practising licence, permitted scope, applicable contract value limits, statutory approvals and project requirements.
